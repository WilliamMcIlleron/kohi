01 — Kōhī

Kōhī means coffee in Japanese. It is also a door into something broader — mindful hospitality, carefully made matcha, and the deep-listening tradition of the jazz kissa.

02 — The room

Raw plaster, warm wood, and a pair of speakers that earn their place.

Kōhī is a café, a micro-gallery and a listening room, rooted in the quiet dialogue between Japanese restraint and warm African design. At the heart of the space sit the turntables, where selectors spin vinyl with the same calm precision that defines everything else we do.

Globally informed, grounded in Joburg's creative soil — this is where Japanese craftsmanship, jazu, and African hospitality meet.
